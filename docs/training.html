Training and development

Practical workshops, leadership and mentoring for parents, teachers, young people and community groups across KwaDukuza and the iLembe District.

Equipping people to thrive

Good intentions go further with the right skills. Through workshops, mentoring and one-on-one time, we give people practical tools they can use straight away at school, at work, at home and in their community.

What we offer

  • Workshops for educators, parents and community groups
  • Leadership and mentoring programmes
  • Jumpstart work-readiness training for young people
  • Free parent workshops at Breakthrough Centre Academy

Out in our schools and communities

Our co-founder Jessica Nair spends a lot of time in local schools and churches, sharing what 36 years with children and families have taught us.

  • Parent workshops: in 2026 she presented two workshops to parents at Radha Roopsingh Primary School on how to monitor and support their children's learning and build strong reading habits at home.
  • Boys Talk and Girls Talk: on 28 August 2026 she spoke to learners at the school's Boys Talk and Girls Talk sessions.
  • Women's conferences: she was a guest speaker at the Bloom Women's Conference hosted by Sanctuary of Hope on 29 August 2026.
  • Children's ministry leaders: we take our Kidz Club leaders to training days and worship experiences, so the next generation of leaders keeps growing.

Would you like a workshop or a speaker for your school, church or group? Get in touch.
