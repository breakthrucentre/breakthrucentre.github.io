Marriage officer

We create heartfelt, personal wedding ceremonies, traditional or non-traditional, in KwaDukuza and on the Dolphin Coast, and register your marriage at Home Affairs.

Book your ceremony

Celebrate your love story

We work with you to make your day meaningful and memorable, from beach ceremonies to evening receptions.

What to bring for registration

Clear photocopies of IDs

  • Bride and groom
  • 2 witnesses

We will have them certified. IDs must be photocopies, not printed copies of photos.

  • Card ID: front and back on the same page
  • Book ID: the complete open front, photocopied

Photos

3 colour photos each of the bride and groom, preferably 4 x 4 cm.

Marrying with a pre-nuptial agreement

A letter from your attorney, on their letterhead, with both your and your partner's details, stating that a pre-nuptial agreement is in place. This must be done before the date of registration.

If your partner is not a South African citizen

  • A "letter of no impediment" from their country of origin or embassy
  • A valid, current visa
  • An appointment for an interview with an immigration official at Home Affairs

On the day of registration you will receive

  • A valid marriage certificate
  • A copy of your marriage register
  • A copy of your witness register
