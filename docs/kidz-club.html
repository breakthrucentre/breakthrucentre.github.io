Kidz Club

Every Sunday from 8 to 10am, Kidz Club brings fun, care and encouragement to children in Etete, Groutville and the surrounding KwaDukuza area.

Sundays
8 to 10am

At Breakthrough Centre, 170 Old Main Road, Etete. All children in our community are welcome.

Kidz Club is where Breakthrough Centre began back in 1990: a children's club giving young people a positive place to be.

Meet Larry!

When Covid-19 forced a temporary closure, we kept the fun going with daily educational programmes on Ballito TV. You can still watch the episodes on YouTube as the Laugh Out Larry Show, starring Larry the puppet.
