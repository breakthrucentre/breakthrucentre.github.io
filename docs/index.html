Community centre and preschool in Groutville, KwaDukuza

Breakthrough Centre has helped children, young people and families in Etete rise above hard circumstances since 1990, through our Academy, Kidz Club, social development and practical care.

From a children's club to a community centre

What began on 10 October 1990 as a children's club in borrowed space at a local school has grown into a full community centre and an early childhood development centre, Breakthrough Centre Academy, where children from age two learn to read, count and speak with confidence.

We are a registered non-profit organisation (081-061-NPO) near KwaDukuza and Ballito, serving families across the iLembe District. Our aim is simple: to empower, equip and inspire people to fulfil their God-given potential.

Our vision

  • A transformed city, empowered communities and an educated next generation.
  • Young people trained, nurtured and developed to be responsible in their schools, communities and workplaces.
  • "A new breed without greed": a generation living with righteousness and integrity.
  • A strategic response to poverty, crime, HIV/Aids and today's challenges.

Our mission

  • Reduce the effects of crime and poverty through empowerment, social development and life skills.
  • Provide a caring, supportive and safe environment for children and youth.
  • Run awareness and support programmes that meet present-day challenges.
  • Effect lasting change in lifestyles through ongoing training and mentoring.

Our values

What we do

Education, care and community development under one roof, for every age.

Breakthrough Centre Academy

Quality early childhood education since 2005: Maths, Science, Languages, Life Skills and our "I can read" programme.

Kidz Club

Every Sunday from 8 to 10am: fun, care and encouragement. Home of Larry the puppet.

Social development

Inter-school contests, our annual Child Protection event, Jumpstart work readiness, food hampers and hot meals.

Marriage officer

Heartfelt, personal wedding ceremonies, traditional or non-traditional, and registration with Home Affairs.

Training and development

Workshops, leadership and mentoring that help people thrive at school, at work and in the community.

Prayer and counselling

A safe, caring space for spiritual growth and emotional healing, walking with people through life's challenges.

How do you change the world?

By touching one little life at a time. Volunteer, partner with us, give or come and visit: there's a place for you at Breakthrough Centre.
