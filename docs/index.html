It started with a children's club

It began with children gathering in front of a little blue house on a hill in Etete. That club never stopped, and today Breakthrough Centre is a full community centre with its own preschool, Breakthrough Centre Academy, where children from the age of two learn to read, count and speak up with confidence.

Breakthrough Centre makes its impact on an ongoing basis, from poverty alleviation programmes to education and empowerment. We see a need and, where possible, we address the need.

We're a registered non-profit organisation (081-061-NPO) in Groutville, between KwaDukuza (Stanger) and Ballito, and we serve families right across the iLembe District. What drives us hasn't changed in 36 years: helping people of every age reach their God-given potential.

A message from our founders

On 10 October 1990, the Lord caused a seed to be planted on the grounds of Etete, Groutville that was destined to germinate, flourish and change the region significantly. The Kidz Club emerged as a powerful children's ministry touching and changing the lives of a rising generation, unveiling the purpose and God-given destiny of precious, tender lives.

Today this ministry has blossomed into a formidable, unstoppable force making an eternal difference in the lives of many.

Our hearts are filled with gratitude as we celebrate 36 incredible years, honouring Abba Father for where He has brought us today. The struggles may have been great, but the impact has surely been greater.

We are thankful to our parents, both physical and spiritual, siblings, our supportive family, our schools, businesses, organisations, covenant partners and friends for your continued support, prayers and generosity. To our Breakthrough Centre Family, Members of the Executive Committee, BCA Staff and our two precious jewels Taldon and Tamsyn: you have sown faithfully, and may your harvest be bountiful. We are reminded of the words of our spiritual father, "Small beginnings of an accurate move will bring you into greater significance."

We celebrate the Lord's awesome favour, goodness and greatness. Surely His hands have been over our lives. In pursuing our dreams, may we be able to carry the capacity to bring light, love and life to the ones we meet.

With much love,
Ps. Trevor and Jessica Nair

Highlights of our year

The annual Kidz Club Christmas party

The highlight of the Breakthrough Centre calendar. There is nothing more rewarding than putting a smile on the face of a child, and our annual Kidz Club party ensures that every child receives a gift for Christmas and a sumptuous treat.

The annual Child Protection Event

The town comes to a standstill as little ones join together with strategic people to make a clarion call declaring an end to child abuse, upholding child protection as one community. The theme "Every child a superhero" sees every child dress up as their favourite superhero, march through the town and speak out against child abuse.

Would you like to partner with us in this exciting event? Contact us for more information.

How we can help you

A preschool, a Sunday club, community projects, weddings, training and a listening ear, all at one address in Etete.

Preschool: Breakthrough Centre Academy

In raising a company of young men and women who would develop into outstanding leaders, the concept of Breakthrough Centre Academy came into being. We also saw the need to keep our little ones in a safe environment where they would thrive, receiving quality education.

Sunday Kidz Club

The most exciting place to be on a Sunday morning, from 8 to 10am. Arts and crafts, sport, Monopoly, Scrabble, the all-time favourite soccer machine and plenty of other games, with Larry the puppet popping in.

Community projects

From poverty alleviation programmes to education and empowerment: the annual Child Protection Event, feeding, blankets and food parcels, hygiene packs and Christmas outreach.

Weddings and marriage officer

A personal ceremony on the beach, in a hall or wherever you choose, and we register your marriage with Home Affairs.

Workshops and training

Practical workshops, leadership and mentoring for parents, teachers, young people and community groups.

Prayer and counselling

Going through something hard? Talk to someone who will listen, pray with you and walk with you.

How do you change the world?

By touching one little life at a time. Give an hour, share a skill, partner with us or simply come and visit. There's a place for you here.

Questions people ask us

Where is Breakthrough Centre?

We're at 170 Old Main Road, Etete, Groutville, Dolphin Coast, KwaZulu-Natal, in Groutville between KwaDukuza (Stanger) and Ballito on the KwaZulu-Natal North Coast. Get directions.

From what age does the Academy take children?

Breakthrough Centre Academy welcomes children from the age of two. See what they learn.

How do I find out about a place for my child?

Send a WhatsApp to 081 775 1488 and we'll tell you about places and registration.

When does Kidz Club meet?

Every Sunday from 8 to 10am at the centre. All children in the community are welcome.

Is Breakthrough Centre a registered non-profit?

Yes. We're a registered non-profit organisation, number 081-061-NPO, and we've served Etete since 1990.
