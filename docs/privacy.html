Privacy and photos

How we look after your information and the photos on this website, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What this website collects

Nothing. This website has no forms, no sign-ups, no cookies and no tracking or advertising tools. Our fonts are stored on our own site. The Google map on our contact page only loads if you choose to show it.

When you tap a WhatsApp or Facebook button, you leave this website and those services' own privacy policies apply. Anything you send us on WhatsApp is used only to reply to you and help you.

Photos of our children and families

The photos on this site celebrate 36 years of Kidz Club, Breakthrough Centre Academy and our community work. We never publish children's surnames, home addresses or other contact details with their photos.

We ask parents and guardians for their permission before we share photos of their children, and you can change your mind at any time.

Would you like a photo removed?

If you, your child or someone in your care appears in a photo on this website and you would like it taken down, please WhatsApp us on 081 775 1488 and tell us which page and photo. We will remove it as soon as we can, with no questions asked.

Our photos and content

The photos and stories on this website belong to Breakthrough Centre, or are shared with permission or with credit to the newspapers and magazines that first published them. Please do not copy, download or reuse them without asking us first.

Questions

For any question about your personal information, contact our Information Officer at Breakthrough Centre, 170 Old Main Road, Etete, Groutville, or WhatsApp 081 775 1488. You also have the right to contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.
