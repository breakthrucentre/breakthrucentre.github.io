Community projects

Beyond the Academy we work alongside local schools, young people and families across KwaDukuza and the iLembe District, through contests, campaigns, training and practical help.

Investing in local schools

Tomorrow's leaders are sitting in today's classrooms, so we invest in local public schools. Our programmes bring teachers, parents, officials and everyone with a stake in education around the same table.

Potential Unleashed: contests and performances

Our inter-school contests in art, poetry and talent help young people find out what they're good at. Children get a stage, an audience and skills that can shape their future. Even during the pandemic we kept the contests going online.

Annual Child Protection Event

Every year local schools and organisations join us to stand together against child abuse, domestic violence and human trafficking. Our own Academy learners lead the Every Child Has a Voice campaign and carry its message to the community.

Jumpstart and relief work

The Jumpstart Programme prepares young people for the world of work.

  • Baby formula distribution: during the 2021 unrest in KZN, we were entrusted with distributing baby formula from Durban to Tugela, so vulnerable families were cared for.
  • Food hampers: regular distributions to families in need.
  • Hot meals: hot meal distribution in the community.
  • Christmas outreach: bringing Christmas cheer to children at a local school.

Partner with us

Schools, businesses and organisations work with us on contests, events and relief. Let's talk.
