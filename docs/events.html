What's on

There's always something happening at the centre. Here's what comes around each year. For exact dates, follow us on Facebook.

  • Kidz Club

    Every Sunday, 8 to 10am.

  • Annual Concert and Graduation

    Every Academy learner on stage, with public speaking, storytelling, dance, drama and singing.

  • Annual Child Protection Event

    Schools and organisations standing together against child abuse, domestic violence and human trafficking.

  • Potential Unleashed contests

    Inter-school contests in art, poetry and talent.

  • Parent meetings and workshops

    Held every term at the Academy.

  • Christmas outreach

    Bringing Christmas cheer to children at a local school.
